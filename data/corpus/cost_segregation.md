# Cost Segregation Studies

## Purpose

Cost segregation accelerates depreciation by reclassifying building components into shorter-lived asset classes (for example, 5-, 7-, or 15-year property) rather than default 27.5- or 39-year real property lives, improving near-term cash flow.

## Study Approach

Source Advisors engineers and tax professionals typically:
1. Review architectural drawings, contractor pay applications, and fixed asset ledgers
2. Perform site inspections when appropriate
3. Allocate costs using accepted engineering-based methodologies
4. Prepare a report suitable for IRS examination support

## Ideal Candidates

- Newly constructed or acquired commercial buildings
- Major renovations or tenant improvements
- Look-back studies for previously placed-in-service properties (subject to accounting method considerations)

## Coordination

Results should be coordinated with the client CPA for Form 3115 / method change implications when required, bonus depreciation elections, and state conformity analysis.
