# Energy Efficiency Incentives — §179D and §45L

## §179D Energy Efficient Commercial Buildings Deduction

§179D provides a deduction for energy-efficient commercial building property that meets applicable efficiency standards. Allocation rules for designers of government-owned buildings are particularly important for architecture and engineering firms.

Source Advisors supports modeling, certification coordination, and deduction quantification aligned to current IRS guidance.

## §45L New Energy Efficient Home Credit

§45L provides a credit for eligible contractors constructing or manufacturing qualifying energy-efficient homes. Eligibility depends on meeting Department of Energy / ENERGY STAR or Zero Energy Ready Home criteria as applicable for the tax year.
