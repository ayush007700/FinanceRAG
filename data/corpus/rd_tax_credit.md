# R&D Tax Credit Advisory

## Overview

The U.S. Research & Development (R&D) Tax Credit rewards qualified research activities and expenses. Source Advisors helps companies and CPA firms identify eligible projects, quantify Qualified Research Expenses (QREs), and prepare contemporaneous documentation.

## Qualified Research Activities

Activities generally must meet the four-part test under IRC §41:
1. Permitted purpose — develop or improve functionality, quality, reliability, or performance of a business component.
2. Technological in nature — rely on principles of engineering, computer science, or physical/biological sciences.
3. Elimination of uncertainty — technical uncertainty existed at the outset.
4. Process of experimentation — evaluate alternatives through modeling, simulation, systematic trial and error, or similar methods.

## Common QRE Categories

- Wages of employees performing, supervising, or directly supporting qualified research
- Supplies used/consumed in qualified research
- Contract research expenses (typically subject to statutory percentage limitations)
- Certain cloud computing costs when used in qualified software development experimentation (facts and circumstances)

## Documentation Best Practices

Source Advisors recommends project narratives, time tracking aligned to experiments, organizational charts, and technical interviews with engineers. Strong documentation reduces exam risk and supports credit sustainability.
